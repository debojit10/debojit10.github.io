How to read this dashboard

  • Contribution = revenue − media spend. It is not gross profit; COGS, fulfilment and marketplace fees are not in this dataset.
  • ROAS = attributed revenue ÷ spend. CPA = spend ÷ purchases. CTR = clicks ÷ impressions. CVR = purchases ÷ clicks.
  • Campaign/ad-set names are not unique across campaigns. This dashboard keys on campaign + adset + date; never aggregate on adset alone.
  • Date range, campaign and objective filters drive every chart, KPI and the downloadable CSV.
