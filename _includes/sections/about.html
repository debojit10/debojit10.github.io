About

I'm an ML Engineer at Rivi, where I own the LLM layer behind our AI products — agent prompts, retrieval, and the evaluation and observability that tell us when any of it drifts. Most of what I build is the unglamorous half: a versioned prompt system serving 7 tenants across 2 channels and 3 environments, a RAG microservice that keeps answers grounded, and per-tenant cost tracing on Langfuse and Braintrust.

The work I care most about is measurement. An LLM-as-judge evaluation pipeline I built baselined our AI services and pinpointed their failure modes, which moved response quality 26%. Once there was a number to defend, replacing GPT-4o with fine-tuned, self-hosted Mistral-7B and Llama-2-13B became a decision rather than a gamble — and cut inference cost 60%.

Earlier I worked on NLP and large-scale data at Shortlyst AI, where I indexed 30M+ profiles and fine-tuned DistilBERT to fill in what the data was missing, and at Locobuzz on social-listening analytics. I hold a Diploma in Data Science from IIT Madras.

Away from a screen I'm usually with my cats, in the gym, or playing soccer.
