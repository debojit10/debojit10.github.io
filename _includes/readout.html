Two kinds: scale a segmented strip — how much of a known range the score fills delta two bars — before against after, on a known maximum GOVERNING RULE: no readout without two real values and a title naming them. Both kinds take their numbers straight from projects.yml. A decorative bar on an unmeasured result would be fabricated telemetry, which for someone whose positioning is "I keep systems honest" is the worst available failure. This is why the four hero metrics have no readout: we know the response-quality gain was 26% but not the baseline it moved from, "30K+ chats" has no denominator, and "7 tenants" has no cap. REDUNDANT CUES: --accent against --rule measures only 1.26:1 in light and 1.59:1 in dark, so fill is never signalled by colour alone. The lit layer is 14px tall against a 6px track, and the delta bars differ by outline-versus-solid as well as by width.
